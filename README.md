## About Me

I am a Software Engineer with a completed B.S. in Software Engineering and hands on experience across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI (Latent Diffusion Models), Computer Vision, IoT and Full Stack Development. I love turning research ideas into real, working products, whether that means flashing firmware onto a microcontroller, training a diffusion model, or shipping a polished web app.

I currently serve as **Chief Technology Officer at HISN-Qararix**, a startup ranked in the Top 10 of Accelerate Punjab, an incubation and acceleration program run by the Punjab Information Technology Board. Before that, I worked as an AI Engineer at NASTP Alpha, contributing to product development and technical delivery within the NASTP ecosystem.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering from Fatima Jinnah Women University, Rawalpindi (2022 to 2026), with coursework spanning Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Software Architecture, Cloud Computing, Computer Vision and Human Computer Interaction.

### What I Work On

- Designing and training deep learning models, from CNNs and Vision Transformers to LSTM, GRU and attention based architectures
- Building generative AI pipelines around Latent Diffusion Models and Stable Diffusion for image to image transformation
- Engineering IoT systems that combine embedded firmware with cloud connected, AI powered monitoring platforms
- Shipping full stack products with React, FastAPI, Django and ASP.NET Core, backed by secure, production ready architecture
- Automating workflows with n8n, connecting OpenAI APIs to Gmail, Google Sheets and Airtable
